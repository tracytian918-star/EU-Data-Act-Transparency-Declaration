About Surplife

Learn how to request and manage your data:

Step 1 – View your basic account data (in-app, no request needed)

Log into the Surplife mobile app → go to "Settings" or "Personal Profile". Here you can see your profile, the list of bound devices, device status, and configured automation scenes.

Step 2 – Request a detailed data export (e.g., historical logs)

Send a request from your registered email address to: support@magichue.net Complete identity verification (and agree to the Terms of Service) The support team will then provide you with a copy of the relevant data.

Note: If you're a buyer trying to delete rather than export data

You can do it directly in-app via Settings → Account Management → Delete Account — no email request required.

Can I recover my data after deleting my account?

No, when you delete your account, all data associated with it is immediately and permanently deleted from our servers and cannot be recovered. Deletions cannot be undone by anyone, no matter the circumstances..
